The problem

You run a complex operation with a notebook and a calculator.

Maple production has more variables than most small businesses, and most tools treat it like a hobby.

01

No map of your bush

You know every tree by feel, but when a new worker shows up or a line goes down, there’s no reference. Everything is in your head.

02

No signal in the bush

The internet is useless when you’re standing next to a tap hole with a problem that needs answering now. Your expert needs to work offline.

03

No diagnosis

You know your yield is down but not why. Vacuum leak? Tree stress? Over-tapped zone? You’re troubleshooting blind with no structured tool.

04

Hidden waste and guesswork

Without RO tracking, DE calculations and evaporation data, you don’t know your real cost per gallon, or where to cut it.

05

Boiling buddy sap without knowing it

Late-season sap can look perfectly clear in the tank. You don’t find out it’s gone to buddy sap until the syrup comes out dark and off-flavor, and by then it’s too late to pull your taps.

Brix trend and buddy sap alert

Don’t lose your grade to bad timing.

Buddy sap comes from trees that have begun to bud. It looks like good sap in the tank, and the syrup it makes is darker, cloudier and off-flavor. Log one Brix reading a day and SweetRun plots the trend. A drop of more than 30% from your peak, with no freeze left in the forecast, triggers a warning while you can still act on it.

Buddy sap detectedExample

Drop−33%

Tomorrow: Hi 54° / Lo 41°. Score 18, no freeze. Syrup made now will be darker and may carry an off-flavor that drops your grade from Golden Delicate toward Dark Robust.

Boil everything in your tanks today. Pull taps when buds start to swell.

SugarSage

Maple answers with no signal.

40+ research-backed entries across 11 categories: sap biology, vacuum, RO, evaporation, grading, tree health and troubleshooting. Sourced from the UVM Proctor Maple Research Center, Cornell Maple Program, USDA Forest Service and IMSI research. No internet required, ever.

Why does maple sap flow in spring?

Driven by freeze-thaw stem pressure. CO₂ dissolves at night creating tension, then re-expands by day, building up to 30 psi of outward pressure…

My vacuum is lower than expected. How do I find the problem?

Work systematically: isolate mainline sections one at a time, walk lines at dawn, and inspect drop lines first. Freeze cracks and UV brittleness cluster there…

What concentration should I target with my RO?

Most operations target 8 to 12°Brix for single-pass. Going above 16°Brix risks sucrose crystallization. UVM sensory studies show minimal flavor impact up to 16°Brix…
