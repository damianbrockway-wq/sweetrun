Why these are free

This math belongs to every sugarmaker. The formulas come from published research (UVM Proctor, Cornell Maple Program, USDA), and hiding them behind a paywall would be silly. Use them, bookmark them, share them with your maple club.

SweetRun, the app behind these pages, is the part you pay for: an offline-first production system that maps your sugarbush, logs every run and batch, scores your sap-run forecast, tracks sap freshness, and shows you your real cost per gallon. It works with zero cell bars in the woods, needs no account, and your data never leaves your phone.
